Happy 27th Birthday Baby ♡

a birthday note for you

Happy 27th birthday baby. I’m still a bit amazed how far we’ve come. We’ve known each other since I was 22 and you were 23… and somehow, through all the chaos of growing up, life, work, and everything in between — we’re still here. I’m really grateful that Allah brought our paths together, and allowed us to build something meaningful, not just in friendship, but also in what we created together. Anggun Pesona, our little baby, feels like something we grew step by step without even realizing how big it would become. So many things happened between us over the years — good days, confusing days, tiring days, and really beautiful ones too. But I’m thankful we didn’t give up on what we were building. I pray that Allah always protects you, makes your path easier, and keeps your heart calm in every phase of life. And I hope you always stay the same kind of person who loves deeply, cares quietly, and gives so much even when no one notices. I know I can be a lot sometimes… overthinking, asking too many questions, being unsure of myself. I’m still learning to be better with that. But thank you for always being patient with me, and for showing me what gentle love feels like. For everything we’ve built and everything we’re still becoming… I’m proud of us. And I really do hope we always find our way back to each other in whatever form life allows. Always.
